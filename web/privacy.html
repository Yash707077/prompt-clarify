Privacy

Last updated 16 August 2026

This is a small free tool made by one person. This page says plainly what happens to your text. No lawyer wrote it, and it is not padded out to look serious.

What is stored on your device

Your five setup answers and a random id are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device except as described below. Clearing your browser data deletes them, and there is no copy anywhere else.

What is sent when you press Improve

  • the prompt you typed
  • your five setup answers
  • the random id, used only to count your daily usage

These go to a server run by the developer on Cloudflare, which forwards them to Google Gemini to produce the rewritten prompt. Google processes that text under their API terms.

Don't paste anything confidential. Client data, passwords, personal details of other people, anything under an NDA. Your text leaves your computer and is processed by Google.

What is kept on the server

Your prompts are not stored. They are passed through and forgotten. The server keeps only counters — how many requests your id, your IP address, and everyone combined have made today. These exist to stop one person exhausting the free allowance, and expire automatically after 48 hours.

No accounts, no tracking

There is no sign-up, no email address, no password. There are no analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts, and no cookies. Nothing about you is sold or shared, because nothing about you is collected.

The browser extension

The extension works the same way. It reads the text in a box only when you click Improve or choose Improve from the right-click menu. It does not read pages in the background, and it has no access to your browsing history.

Children

This tool is not intended for anyone under 13.
